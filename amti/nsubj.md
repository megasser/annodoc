title: nsubj
---

We use `nsubj` for the nominal argument of a finite verb that agrees with (is co-referential) with its morphological subject. A clause can have at most one `nsubj`.

~~~ sdparse
am-ref-4: ውሻው ይጮሃል ።
nsubj(ይጮሃል, ውሻው)
~~~

~~~ sdparse
ti-ref-4: ከልቢ ይነብሕ ።
nsubj(ይነብሕ, ከልቢ)
~~~
